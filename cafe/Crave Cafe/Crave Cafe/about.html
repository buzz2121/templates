Who We Are

Every Flavor Tells a Story

What began as a small kitchen driven by one family's love for cooking has grown into one of London's most cherished dining destinations. At Crave, we believe that great food is more than a meal — it's a memory, a conversation, a moment worth savouring.

Every dish on our menu is crafted from locally sourced, seasonal ingredients by chefs who treat cooking as an art form. We work directly with trusted farms, fisheries, and artisan producers to ensure that what arrives on your plate is nothing short of exceptional. Our commitment to quality extends from the kitchen to the table — where warm hospitality meets an atmosphere designed for celebration.

The Crave Difference

What Sets Us Apart

We don't just serve food — we create experiences. Here's what our guests love most about dining with us.

Farm-to-Table Sourcing

We partner with over 20 local farms and fisheries within a 50-mile radius. Our produce arrives fresh each morning, ensuring the vibrant flavors and peak nutrition that only seasonal ingredients can deliver.

Private Dining & Events

From intimate anniversary dinners to corporate gatherings of up to 60 guests, our private dining rooms offer bespoke menus, dedicated service, and an atmosphere tailored to your occasion.

Artisan Bar & Cellar

Our sommelier-curated wine list features over 120 labels from boutique vineyards across Europe and the New World. Pair that with handcrafted cocktails mixed by our award-winning bartenders.
