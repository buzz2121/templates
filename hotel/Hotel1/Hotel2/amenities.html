Wellness & Spa

The Sanctum Spa

Our 3,000 sq ft Sanctum Spa is a temple of restorative wellness. Drawing inspiration from ancient healing traditions across Asia, Arabia, and the Mediterranean, every treatment is a bespoke ritual.

Featuring 8 private treatment suites, a hydrotherapy pool, a Finnish sauna, a steam room, and a relaxation lounge overlooking the gardens, The Sanctum offers a complete escape from the world.

Culinary Excellence

Aurum Restaurant

Aurum is a Michelin-starred dining experience that celebrates the finest ingredients sourced from local artisan farmers and global culinary partners.

Executive Chef Marcus De Luca presents seasonal tasting menus that evolve with the harvest. Complemented by a curated wine cellar featuring over 400 labels, every dinner is a journey through taste and culture.
