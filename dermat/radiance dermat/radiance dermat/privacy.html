Privacy

At Radiance Derma Clinic, your privacy is our utmost priority. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal and medical information.

1. Information Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us when you book a ritual, sign up for our newsletter, or communicate with our concierge team. This includes your name, contact details, and relevant medical history.

2. Medical Confidentiality

All medical records and skin analysis data are stored in a secure, encrypted system accessible only by Dr. Aisha Sharma and authorized clinical staff. We adhere to international standards of medical data protection.

3. Data Usage

Your information is used solely to provide personalized skin rituals, manage your appointments, and send you relevant skincare insights if you have opted in.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with medical partners if required for your specific treatment and with your explicit consent.
