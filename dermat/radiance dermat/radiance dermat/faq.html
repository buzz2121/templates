Technical & Safety

Is Laser therapy painful?

We utilize advanced Sapphire integrated cooling technology that maintains skin temperature at a constant 4°C during the ritual. This makes the experience virtually painless, often described as a minor cooling sensation.

How is the clinic hygiene maintained?

Radiance operates at **Hospital-Grade Class-100 sterile standards**. All equipment undergoes 4-stage ultrasonic sterilization, and every treatment suite is UV-sanitized for 20 minutes between guests.

Is there downtime after procedures?

Most rituals like our signature Medical Facials have zero downtime. For intense corrective procedures, you might experience minor flaking for 48 hours, for which we provide a complete post-care concierge kit.
