Shani Dev's Rashi Parivartan: 2026 Transit Impact

📅 March 28, 2026 👤 By Pandit Aura 🏷 Transit Update

Saturn, the planet of karma and justice, is preparing for one of the most significant planetary shifts of the decade. In early 2026, Shani Dev will move from the airy sign of Aquarius into the watery depths of Pisces.

This transit marks the end of a 30-year cycle for many signs and the beginning of a profound transformation period. Pisces, being the last sign of the zodiac, represents moksha, liberation, and the subconscious. When the planet of discipline enters this domain, our spiritual foundations are tested.

Impact on Different Rashis

For Aries (Mesh), Shani moves into the 12th house, starting the first phase of Sade Sati. This is a period for introspection and managing expenses. Avoid risky investments.

For Pisces (Meena), Shani enters your 1st house. This is the peak of Sade Sati. While it brings responsibilities and hard work, it also offers the opportunity to build a lasting legacy through sheer discipline.

Remedies for Shani Transit

To mitigate the malefic effects of a heavy Saturn transit, Vedic astrology suggests the following rituals:

  • Lighting a mustard oil lamp (Diya) under a Peepal tree on Saturday evenings.
  • Chanting the Shani Beej Mantra: "Om Pram Preem Proum Sah Shanaye Namah" 108 times.
  • Donating black sesame seeds or iron items to the needy.
  • Being truthful and maintaining high ethical standards in your profession.

Unsure how Shani affects your specific chart?

Every Janam Kundli has different planetary strengths. A generic transit reading may not apply to you if your Shani is exceptionally strong or weak.
