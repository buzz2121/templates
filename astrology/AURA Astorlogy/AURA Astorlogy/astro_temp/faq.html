Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifying your doubts about Vedic Astrology and our consultation process.

✦ How accurate are the predictions?
Vedic Astrology is a high-precision science based on mathematical planetary positions. While the calculations are 100% accurate, interpretation depends on the expertise of the astrologer. We provide guidance based on traditional texts and years of experience.
✦ What information is required for a reading?
To create an accurate Janam Kundli, we need your exact Date of Birth, Time of Birth (with AM/PM), and Place of Birth (City/State). If the time is approximate, please mention it during booking.
✦ Can astrology change my future?
Astrology is like a weather forecast. It tells you when it will rain so you can carry an umbrella. Remedies (Upayas) like Mantras, Gemstones, and Pooja help mitigate negative planetary influences and strengthen positive ones, but Karma remains the core driver of life.
✦ Are online consultations as effective as face-to-face?
Absolutely. The planetary positions remain the same regardless of your physical location. Our video and phone consultations provide the same depth and clarity as an in-person meeting.
✦ How long does a consultation last?
A standard deep-dive consultation lasts between 45 to 60 minutes. This allows enough time for chart analysis, answering your specific questions, and discussing remedies.
