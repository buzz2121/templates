The House Chronology

The Legacy of
Brilliance

A journey through the architectural mathematical purity of light.

EST.

1924

ORIGIN

Place Vendôme, Paris

1984
The Founding

The Paris Atelier

Julian Sorella Gems opens the first sanctuary on the Rue de la Paix. A dedication to the mathematical precision of stone setting and the architectural flow of light began here, setting the standard for the decades to come.

1995
High Artistry

Royal Recognition

The house is commissioned to design the 'Soleil' tiara for European nobility. This milestone cemented Sorella Gems's status as a global authority in high-jewellery artistry and royal provenance.

2012
Conscious Luxury

Ethical Mastery

Leading the industry into a new era. Sorella Gems becomes one of the first maisons to implement 100% GIA-certified, ethically traceable stone sourcing across its entire global portfolio.

2026
The Future

Global Concierge

Bringing the Place Vendôme experience to the palm of every collector. The expansion into a global digital flagship era allows the Maison to assist its community with unprecedented intimacy.
